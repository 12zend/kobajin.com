    Memory & privacy

    ⌂

    Runs in your browser

    The model is downloaded to this device and replies are generated locally. Your chat messages are not sent to a server.

    ✳

    A few reviewed memories

    kobajinAI can use a small set of reviewed memories when they help answer a question. Each reply says whether it looked one up and what it found. It should say when it does not know a personal detail.

    ▤

    Conversation history

    Your recent chats are stored in this browser so you can come back to them. A message you sent before the model was ready, or while the page closed mid-reply, is kept and answered next time. Clear them here at any time.

    ≡

    Feed

    Feed posts, replies, and their check logs are kept in this browser only. The feed fetches article excerpts from Japanese Wikipedia; nothing you write is sent there.
